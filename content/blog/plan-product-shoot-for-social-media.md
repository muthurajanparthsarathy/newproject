Most small brands post in bursts: lots of content after a shoot, then weeks of silence while everyone scrambles for something to post. The fix isn't shooting more often — it's **planning one shoot so it covers the whole month**.

Here's the method we use when we plan shoots for brands.

## Start from where the content will be used

Before thinking about props or locations, list every place the photos and videos will appear in the next month:

- Instagram and Facebook posts (square or 4:5)
- Reels and Stories (vertical 9:16)
- Ads, which need a few versions of the same idea to test
- Your website — banners and product pages (horizontal)
- WhatsApp catalogue and status

This tells you which **shapes** you need. Shooting vertical and horizontal versions of key shots on the day is far easier than cropping later.

## Build a shot list in five groups

A good shot list covers different *reasons* for a customer to stop scrolling.

### 1. Hero shots

Clean, well-lit photos of each product on its own. These go on your website, catalogue and ads.

### 2. Details

Close-ups of texture, ingredients, stitching, packaging — whatever shows quality.

### 3. In use

Someone using, wearing, cooking with or unboxing the product. These usually perform best in ads because customers can picture themselves using it.

### 4. Behind the scenes

Your team, your workspace, how the product is made or packed. This is what makes a local brand feel real and trustworthy.

### 5. Short video moments

Record 5–15 second clips while you shoot photos: a pour, a reveal, a spin, a reaction. Each one can become a reel, a Story or the opening of an ad.

## Plan the month before you shoot

Sketch a simple calendar and assign each shot to a week:

| Week | Theme | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch / hero | Hero photos, one product reel |
| 2 | How it's used | In-use photos, a how-to reel |
| 3 | Behind the scenes | Team and making-of photos, BTS reel |
| 4 | Proof and offer | Customer photo (with permission), offer post, best-performing reel boosted as an ad |

Now you know exactly what to capture — and nothing gets forgotten on the day.

## On the day

- **Shoot in batches**: all hero shots first, then details, then in-use. Changing setups less often saves hours.
- **Record extra takes** of anything that might become a reel. Five seconds of extra footage costs nothing.
- **Keep the same products and colours** across the shoot so the month looks consistent on your feed.
- **Note captions as you go** — the story behind a product is easiest to write while it's in front of you.

## After the shoot

Edit and name files by week and format (for example `week2-inuse-vertical-01.jpg`) so whoever posts can find them in seconds. Schedule what you can in advance, and keep a few spare photos for days when something unexpected comes up.
