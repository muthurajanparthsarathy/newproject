When someone in Coimbatore searches for "salon near me" or "bakery in Saravanampatti", Google usually shows a map with a few businesses before any website. Those listings come from **Google Business Profile** — and setting one up is free.

If your business serves customers in person, this is one of the most valuable hours you can spend on marketing.

## What a Google Business Profile does

Your profile is the panel that appears in Google Search and Google Maps with your:

- Business name, category and location on the map
- Opening hours and phone number
- Photos of your shop, products and team
- Customer reviews and your replies
- Buttons to call, get directions or visit your website

People can decide to visit you without ever opening your website — so the profile needs to be complete and correct.

## How to set it up

1. Go to **google.com/business** and sign in with a Google account you'll keep long-term (not a staff member's personal account).
2. Search for your business name. If it already exists, choose **Claim this business**; if not, add it.
3. Pick the **primary category** that best describes what you do — "Beauty salon", not just "Shop". This matters more than most people think.
4. Enter your address exactly as customers would find it, and place the pin accurately on the map.
5. Add your phone number and website.
6. **Verify** the listing. Google will offer one or more ways to do this — such as a phone call, a short video of your premises, or a postcard. Follow whichever options it shows you.

## Fill in everything

A half-finished profile looks like a closed business. Once you're verified, complete:

- **Hours**, including special hours for festivals and holidays
- **Services or products**, with prices if you're comfortable sharing them
- **Description** — a few honest sentences about what you do and who you serve
- **Photos** — the outside of your shop (so people recognise it), the inside, your products and your team

> Use real photos taken at your business. Stock images make customers trust a listing less, not more.

## Reviews: ask, reply, and never fake them

Reviews are the part of your profile customers read most closely.

- **Ask happy customers** — a simple WhatsApp message with your review link works well. You'll find the link in your profile under "Ask for reviews".
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad. A calm, helpful reply to a complaint often impresses future customers more than a five-star review.
- **Never buy reviews or post fake ones.** It breaks Google's rules, can get your listing suspended, and customers can usually tell.

## Keep it active

Google favours profiles that look alive. Once a week or so:

- Add a new photo
- Post an update or offer
- Answer any questions people have asked
- Check that your hours and phone number are still correct

## Make it match your website

Your business name, address and phone number should be written **exactly the same way** on your Google profile, your website, Instagram and anywhere else you're listed. Consistent details help Google trust that the information is correct.
